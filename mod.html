Lotus Maps Beta

Turn-by-turn directions along the Highway, right inside Minecraft. Pick a stop, and Lotus Maps shows the way on screen, reads the turns out loud and draws the route in front of you, like the Map & Directions page but without leaving the game.

Lotus Maps 1.0.0-beta.1 Fabric · Minecraft 26.1.2 · 131 KB · client-side only
Download the mod

What it does

Directions bannerThe next turn, how far away it is, and the time to your stop, at the top of the screen.
Voice directions“In 1,000 blocks, turn left onto Lotus Highway one oh five.” Callouts at 1,000 and 150 blocks.
Route lineThe ice ahead of you is coloured blue along your route, diagonal intersections included.
JourneyMap supportRoads with highway shields, paths, stops and your route on the minimap and full map.
Right-click to goRight-click the JourneyMap full map for “Navigate here” or “Navigate to stop”.
Always up to dateRoads and stops load from this website and the live map’s claims every time you play.

Install

  1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 26.1.2 (or make a Fabric 26.1.2 instance in Prism, Modrinth App or CurseForge).
  2. Add Fabric API to your mods folder.
  3. Put lotusmaps-1.0.0-beta.1.jar in the same mods folder.
  4. Optional: add JourneyMap to see the Highway on your map, and Mod Menu for the settings screen.
  5. Join Lotus, warp with /pw Highway click to copy and type /lotusmaps.

Commands

Use /lotusmaps or the short /lm. Press Tab after go to pick a stop.

CommandWhat it does
/lm go <stop>Directions to a stop, e.g. /lm go Atlantis
/lm goto <x> <z>Directions to coordinates
/lm stopEnd navigation (it also ends by itself when you arrive)
/lm listEvery stop, nearest first
/lm voice on|offSpoken directions
/lm line on|offThe route line in the world
/lm hud on|offThe directions banner
/lm map on|offThe Highway on JourneyMap (or use the Highway button on the full map)
/lm reloadLoad the newest roads and stops right now

About the beta

  • Voice directions use Windows’ built-in voices. On Mac they use say; on Linux, spd-say if installed.
  • Directions follow the same roads and rules as the Map & Directions page, so routes match the website.
  • Client-side only: nothing is needed on the server, and it doesn’t change how you play. It only shows the way.
  • Settings: with Mod Menu installed, open Mods → Lotus Maps → Configure (also works mid-game from the pause menu). Changes apply instantly. Without Mod Menu, use the /lm commands or edit .minecraft/config/lotusmaps.json.
  • Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us on the Forum.
